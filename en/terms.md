## Article 2 (Description of the Service)

1. The Service extracts text on the screen of your Android device using OCR, or converts audio being played on your device into text using speech recognition (audio translation (Audio)), and displays the translation of that text as an overlay. The iOS version (iPhone) offers audio translation (Audio) only: it receives the audio played by other apps through iOS screen recording and shows the translation in a Picture-in-Picture floating window. Translation and speech recognition are performed by models on servers operated by the Developer or by an external translation API (large language models); which is used is determined automatically based on the type of processing and server availability (see the Privacy Policy for details).
2. A network connection is required to use the Service. In particular, because audio translation (Audio) performs speech recognition on a server, it cannot be used without a network connection or while the Developer's servers are down.
3. The form, specifications, and features of the Service may be changed or discontinued without prior notice to users. However, changes to the allowances set out in these Terms and any other changes to these Terms are governed by Article 9.

## Article 3 (Fees)

1. The Service offers two plans: a free plan and a paid plan.

2. **Free plan**: You may use the Service within an allowance of 30 credits per day. Credits are shared across screen translation, real-time translation (Live), and audio translation (Audio), and are consumed as follows.

   - Screen translation (Page / Manga): 1 credit per translation
   - Real-time translation (Live): 1 credit per 30 seconds (2 credits per minute). Credits are consumed for the time Live is running, whether or not subtitles are displayed
   - Audio translation (Audio): 1 credit per 30 seconds (2 credits per minute)

   Accordingly, if you spend all of your credits on the time-based features (Live or Audio), the daily maximum is 15 minutes in total. Credits are counted per device and reset when the date changes on your device (at midnight in the time zone set on your device). The number of credits and the consumption rates may change in accordance with Article 9.

   Because the iOS version offers audio translation (Audio) only, the free plan on iOS allows up to 15 minutes of audio translation in total per day. Usage time resets when the date changes on your device. Free-plan usage is counted per device and is not combined or carried over between the Android and iOS versions.

3. **Paid plan**: You may use the Service within an allowance of 960 credits per day (32 times the free plan). Credits are shared and consumed in the same way as in the preceding paragraph. Accordingly, if you spend all of your credits on the time-based features (Live or Audio), the daily maximum is 8 hours in total, and if you use them only for screen translation (Page / Manga), the daily maximum is 960 translations. Because the iOS version offers audio translation (Audio) only, the paid plan on iOS allows up to 8 hours of audio translation in total per day.

   As with the free plan, paid-plan credits are counted per device and reset when the date changes on your device (at midnight in the time zone set on your device). If you use one subscription on more than one device, the allowance of 960 credits per day applies to each device separately. Unused credits do not carry over to the next day.
